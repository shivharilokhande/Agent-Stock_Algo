## Definitions

- **PULLBACK_EMA20** (swing): TREND_PB analogue: strong uptrend, recently stretched >=6% above EMA20, today dips to
    EMA20 (low within 1%) and closes back above it as a green candle.
- **BREAKOUT_55** (swing): 55-day high breakout on >=1.5x volume, strong close, above SMA200, not overextended.
- **RECLAIM_SMA50** (swing): VWAP_RECLAIM analogue on daily: closes back above SMA50 inside a rising long-term trend.
- **RSI2_DIP** (swing): Short-term oversold (RSI2 < 10) inside an uptrend; exit on close above SMA5.
- **TREND_TEMPLATE** (long): Long-term trend template: 50>150>200 stack, 200 rising, >=30% off 52w low, within 25%
    of 52w high, 12-month relative strength in the top 30% of NIFTY 500. Fires on the first
    day the full template is true after 20+ days of not being true.
- Exit **R2**: stop / 2R target / 20-day time exit
- Exit **TRAIL**: stop, then 3xATR chandelier trail off highest close / 60 days
- Exit **MA5**: exit next open after a close above SMA5 / stop / 10 days
- Exit **LONG**: 15% stop, exit next open after a close below SMA150
